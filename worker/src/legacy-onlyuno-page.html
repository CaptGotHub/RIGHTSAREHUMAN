Copyrights for Humans

Being a Declaration that Copyrights Belong to People, Not the Robots

Proclaimed this 8th day of November, 2026

✍ Sign the Proclamation ↓

Takes 30 seconds · 3 quick steps

❦

Whereas every song, story, photograph, painting and line of code worth protecting began as a spark in a human mind; and whereas machines now produce words and pictures by the billion, at no cost and without rest; and whereas the purpose of copyright has always been to reward and encourage people who create;

We, the undersigned humans, do hereby proclaim:

  1. Copyright is a human right. It exists to protect the work of human creators, and should remain in the hands of people.
  2. Tools are not authors. A brush, a camera, a guitar or a computer program may help make a work, but the credit and the rights belong to the person who made it.
  3. Creators deserve a say. People should know when their work is used to train machines, and have a voice in how it is used.
  4. Humans first, always. As technology changes, the law should change with it, but never at the expense of the people who make culture.
