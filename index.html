Our First Priority

Prepare legislation to protect human personhood.

As AI and robotics advance, we must prepare before the legal distinction between human beings and machines becomes confused. We ask lawmakers and the public to begin work on clear safeguards that protect human rights and distinguish human personhood from any legal status that might someday be considered for artificial systems.

Human rights must not be diminished or displaced by possible future rights for robots. Federal and state legislation—and, where appropriate, constitutional clarification—should protect human dignity, freedom, self-determination and human accountability for generations to come.

This is a call to prepare legislation, not a claim that machines currently have human personhood or that a bill is already pending.

Whereas human rights belong to human beings, and intelligent machines may increasingly imitate human conversation, decisions and creative work, we believe the time to prepare protections is now—not after confusion begins.

We proclaim:

  1. Humans first, always. Human dignity, freedom and fundamental rights must remain secure as technology advances.
  2. Personhood must be clear. Laws should distinguish human beings from artificial systems, whatever status may one day be considered for machines.
  3. Human accountability matters. Decisions about human rights must remain accountable to people and human-led institutions.
  4. Human creativity matters. Human originality, expression, skill and judgment deserve recognition and protection.
  5. Prepare now. We invite lawmakers and the public to consider clear federal, state and, where appropriate, constitutional safeguards.

Our purpose: We do not reject technology. We ask that the rights of people remain unmistakable and secure.
